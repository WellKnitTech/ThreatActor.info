## Description

Adversaries may attempt to exfiltrate data over a USB connected physical device. In certain circumstances, such as an air-gapped network compromise, exfiltration could occur via a USB device introduced by a user. The USB device could be used as the final exfiltration point or to hop between otherwise disconnected systems. [A USB data exfiltration case study](https://www.cell.com/heliyon/fulltext/S2405-8440(23)00232-3)
