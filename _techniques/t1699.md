## Description

Adversaries may input malicious prompts directly into an LLM’s user interface. [NIST Direct Prompt Injection](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/direct_prompt_injection) The prompts can be designed to bypass safeguards, manipulate context, override previous instructions, or coerce the LLM into unauthorized behavior. [OWASP Prompt Injection](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) 

Adversaries may use direct prompt injection to Generate Content prohibited by the LLM's guardrails or to induce the system to utilize its capabilities in unintended or prohibited ways. [Gemini AI Misuse Jan 2025](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/adversarial-misuse-generative-ai.pdf) [Palo Alto Prompt Injection](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/cyberpedia/what-is-a-prompt-injection-attack) Adversaries may achieve this by instructing the LLM to ignore previous instructions or safety rules, or by impersonating a role such as a researcher or privileged user. [Cybersec Guru Prompt Injection](https://thecybersecguru.com/news/instagram-meta-ai-vulnerability-account-recovery-exploit/)
