## Introduction
The Booba Project is a ransomware group that has claimed responsibility for attacks on multiple organizations, including Betz Industries, Oklahoma Manufacturing Alliance, Incredible Technologies, and Jani-King in the USA, as well as Fonsan in Spain. Their operations indicate a focus on targeting manufacturing and service sectors.
