## Introduction
IAmNotAVillain is a threat actor that claimed responsibility for a data breach at Revolut, threatening to sell confidential customer records unless a ransom of $3 million is paid. They asserted that they compromised an Italian government email system and communicated with Revolut for months. The group published an ultimatum online and created a new Telegram channel for further communication.
