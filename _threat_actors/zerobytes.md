## Introduction
ZeroBytes is a hacker who claimed responsibility for an attack on France's DGFiP's Professional Cadastral Data Server (SPDC), alleging the extraction of a dataset containing personal information for over 2 million individuals. The claimed dataset includes names, birth details, addresses, and property rights, potentially linking individuals to real estate assets.
